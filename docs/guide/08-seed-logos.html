Chapter 9The company list and the logos

HiresHere can only show jobs from companies it knows. pipeline/seed.py builds and validates the list of boards; pipeline/logos.py finds each company's website and icon.

Where the companies come from

Two open lists, both credited in data/CREDITS.md:

  • remoteintech/remote-jobs (ISC licence): a curated list of remote-friendly companies with their careers page.
  • kalil0321/ats-scrapers (MIT licence): CSV files mapping thousands of companies to their slug on each ATS.

seed build joins them: first it reads the careers URL (a jobs.lever.co/acme link reveals ATS and slug directly), then matches the company name against the ATS lists, and finally guesses slugs with one cheap request each.

{{py pipeline/seed.py probe}}

Validation and the noise filter

seed validate fetches every candidate once and keeps those that answer and have jobs. Boards added from the big inventories (not hand-curated) must also pass noise(): at least 5% of their jobs must look remote, or they must have two or more entry-level openings that are not office-only. Template spam (hundreds of jobs with a handful of titles) is dropped too.

{{py pipeline/seed.py entry_level}} {{py pipeline/seed.py noise}}
Choosing companies for juniors

In October 2026, 162 companies were added from a random sample of 2,800 boards, chosen for intern, junior and graduate openings. After the first classification run, the 44 whose jobs were all office-only were removed. source_of_entry = ats-scrapers+entry marks them in boards.csv.

Logos (pipeline/logos.py)

For each company with a website, the script downloads the page, parses its <link rel="icon"> tags and picks the best one (an apple-touch-icon first, then the largest declared size). Logos are saved under site/public/logos/ and committed, so pages never load images from third-party sites.

{{py pipeline/logos.py Icons}}

Many boards have no website in the lists, so the script first reads it from the company's own board page (each ATS shows a "home page" link in a known place), rejecting links that point to a hiring system or a placeholder domain:

{{py pipeline/logos.py HOME}}

A SKIP set lists icons that came back unusable (white on white, empty, corrupt), so those companies show their initials on a coloured tile instead.
