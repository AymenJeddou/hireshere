Chapter 20Design decisions and trade-offs

Every system is a set of choices. Here are the important ones in HiresHere, what was chosen, and what it cost. The full dated log is docs/DECISIONS.md.

DecisionChosenAlternativeWhy
Where jobs come fromPublic ATS feeds and boards that allow reuseScraping LinkedIn or IndeedLegal and stable; feeds are structured JSON; the same postings as the companies' own pages.
DatabaseSQLite file, carried between runs as a release assetA hosted PostgresFree, zero setup, fast for 25,000 rows; the pipeline runs once a day in one place.
AI's roleExtract facts once; rules decide per countryAsk the AI "is this open to Tunisia?" per country243 countries at no extra cost; rules are testable and explainable; changing a rule needs no AI calls.
CautionLow confidence capped at "likely"; quote must be verbatimTrust the modelA wrong "You can apply" costs the user an evening and the site its credibility.
WebsiteStatic Astro pages, data in JSON, search in the browserA server-rendered app with a databaseFree hosting, fast everywhere, nothing to keep running.
Job pagesRendered on request by a Function from shardsPrerender every job36,000 pages exceed the free 20,000-file limit.
Functions scopeOnly /jobs/* and /api/*, with a browser fallbackMiddleware on every requestThe free 100,000 calls a day ran out once; now one call per page view, and the site survives if they run out again.
AI costFree tiers of three providers in parallel, pacedPaid Gemini batch ($1.85 per 4,500 postings)No budget. Trade-off: about 300 to 500 postings a day, so big additions take a few days.
Queue orderNewest firstOldest firstOldest first left today's jobs waiting and alerts empty.
PublishingRight after fetching, and again after the AIOnce at the endFresh data is live in 3 minutes instead of 40.
AccountsNone; saved jobs and profile in localStorageUser accountsLess to build and secure; nothing personal stored on servers.
EmailOne digest a day at most; welcome email; HMAC unsubscribeInstant emails per jobRespectful of inboxes and of Resend's 100 a day.
Dependenciesstdlib + httpx + pydantic; Astro onlyFrameworks for scraping, ORM, UI kitsLess to learn, update and secure.
Open sourcePublic repo, MIT licence; database encryptedPrivateVisibility for the project and its author; descriptions stay protected.

Known limits

  • Free AI quotas cap how many new postings are read per day; a large batch of new companies takes several days to process.
  • Verdicts are automated estimates; the site always says to confirm with the employer.
  • The Arabic interface text and country names still need a native review.
  • GitHub starts scheduled runs late on busy days.
  • The sitemap (about 39,000 URLs) is approaching the 50,000-URL limit per file and will need splitting.
