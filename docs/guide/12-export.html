Chapter 13Export: from the database to the website's data

pipeline/export.py turns about 25,000 open jobs into the files the site reads: a search index per language, 256 detail shards, and two metadata files. It never exports full descriptions.

Reading the jobs

{{py pipeline/export.py rows}}

Only jobs that are open and have an extraction are exported. The evidence_verified flag is taken out of the stored JSON before rebuilding the Extraction.

Verdicts for every country, compressed

Storing 243 verdicts per job for 12,000 jobs would be 3 million entries. Instead, countries with the same outcome are grouped:

{{py pipeline/export.py groups}}

For a job open in EMEA, the result looks like this: one group "likely, Open across EMEA, which includes {c}" listing about 110 country codes, and a group "*" meaning "every other country". Reasons carry a {c} placeholder where the country name goes, filled in later in the visitor's language. A job no country may see ([]) is not exported at all.

{{py pipeline/export.py for_country}}

Building one job record

{{py pipeline/export.py build}}
  • Office-only jobs are dropped (WORKPLACES).
  • A short label like "EMEA" or "US · CA" (scope_label) summarises who may apply.
  • The quote is shown only if it really appears in the posting and is not a company slogan.
  • Jobs are sorted by the employer's posting date, newest first (when).

Slugs: readable, stable URLs

{{py pipeline/export.py job_slug}}

/jobs/reddit-senior-growth-manager-contract-2a343b: the company and title for humans and search engines, plus 6 hex characters of a SHA-1 of the job id so two jobs with the same title never collide. The first two of those characters choose the job's shard.

The files

{{py pipeline/export.py search_record}} {{py pipeline/export.py write_shards}}

The search index holds only what a card needs, so it stays around 500 KB compressed per language. A job page needs more (location, apply link, time zone, department, similar jobs) and loads only its shard, one 256th of the details.

{{py pipeline/export.py export}}
Why not prerender every job page?

Cloudflare Pages' free plan allows 20,000 files per deployment. 12,000 jobs in 3 languages would be 36,000 pages. So job pages are built on request by a Function from the shards (chapter 17), and everything else is static.
